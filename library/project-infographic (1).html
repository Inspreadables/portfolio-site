Key Project Insights

01

Sequential Beats Single-Tool

Experts consistently recommend chaining Perplexity → Claude rather than relying on either alone. Each tool excels at different stages of the research workflow.

02

Hierarchical Tagging Wins

A 7-level tagging system (domain, function, format, audience, status, language, org) outperforms simple single-level categorization for 1,000+ doc libraries.

03

Automation ≠ Autopilot

Scheduled pipelines still benefit from human review protocols. The best workflow includes a final approval step before publishing to stakeholders.
