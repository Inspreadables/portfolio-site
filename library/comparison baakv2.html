### Key Findings & Evolutionary Path for De Baak 1. **The Scalability Paradox:** Currently, De Baak's growth is linear—to earn more, they must hire more trainers or fill more seats. By adopting the **Designing Your Life (DYL)** model, they can shift to a **Licensing Model**. The 300 trainers shouldn't just be employees; they should be certified practitioners of "The De Baak Method" who pay a royalty to use the brand's tools and platform. 2. **Trainer as "Node":** Inspired by **Substack**, De Baak can provide the infrastructure (Discovery, Payment, CMS) for its trainers to build their own sub-communities. This creates a "hub-and-spoke" model where De Baak captures a percentage of the value generated by its "stars" without having to manage their every session. 3. **From Education to Ecosystem:** Following the **School of Life** example, De Baak can move beyond workshops into "Life Tools." This includes proprietary apps for leadership tracking, physical journals, and B2B subscription portals (Commune model). 4. **Market Capture Prognosis:** By 2030, a traditional training institute risks being commoditized by AI-driven coaching.
