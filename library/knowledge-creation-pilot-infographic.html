💡 Why This Pilot?
Year 1 is an intentional investment phase. By validating the 7-lesson flagship with real users, we de-risk the full €206k Year 1 operating cost and generate evidence for partners, funders, and B2B clients.

Project Purpose & Vision

Vision

Create a research-backed, behavior-change oriented learning environment that integrates deep work, life design, and relational health in a blended format of short videos, tools, and peer learning.

Strategic Intent

The Knowledge Creation Platform will solve a critical gap: mid-career professionals need structured support combining three pillars (productivity, career clarity, relational health) but existing offers are fragmented and either too theoretical or too expensive.

This pilot is the first production of the platform ecosystem, proving that video + tools + community + behavior change metrics drive sustainable engagement and revenue.

Primary Objectives

  • Validate problem-solution fit with real mid-career professionals
  • Test delivery model (async video, tools, optional peer circles)
  • Achieve pilot success metrics (70% completion, NPS 40+)
  • Generate behavior change stories and testimonials
  • Prove business model viability (pricing, CAC, LTV)
  • Create foundation for 10-session flagship and broader portfolio

Secondary Objectives

  • Establish brand and market positioning
  • Build first alumni network for referrals
  • Create case studies and testimonials for B2B
  • Test platform and tooling stack
  • Validate instructor credibility and delivery style
  • Position for Year 1 institutional partnerships
Success Means...
  • A validated flagship course that professionals actively pay for and complete
  • Measurable behavior change (work habits, decisions, relationship shifts)
  • Sustainable unit economics with clear path to profitability by Year 2
  • A replicable model that can scale to 10-session, 30-lesson, and beyond
  • Institutional credibility for partnerships and B2B sales

24-Week Roadmap: IDEO Design Thinking Phases

DISCOVER
Weeks 1-3

Research & Understand

Activities: Conduct 8-12 interviews with target audience | Scan 5 competing platforms | Observe analogous solutions (Duolingo, Peloton, etc.)

Deliverables: 2-3 user personas | Insight summary with 3-5 key themes | Design challenge framing

Key Output: Validated problem statement and audience understanding

INTERPRET
Weeks 4-5

Frame Opportunities

Activities: Team workshop to cluster themes | Generate 3-5 "How might we" statements | Define pilot success criteria

Deliverables: 1-page challenge statement | Success metrics framework | Draft value proposition

Key Output: Clear design brief and metrics alignment

IDEATE
Weeks 6-8

Design the Flagship

Activities: Co-create 7-lesson structure | Develop script beats and reflection prompts | Sketch user journey

Deliverables: 30-40 page instructional design doc | Script outlines (not word-for-word) | Storyboard of experience

Key Output: Approved pilot design, ready for production

EXPERIMENT
Weeks 9-18

Produce & Build

Sub-phases:

• Pre-Prod (9-10): Finalize lesson briefs, scout location, tech check
• Production (11): 2-day shoot, 2-3 camera angles, lavalier audio
• Post-Prod (12-15): Edit 7 videos, audio mastering, graphics, captions
• Platform (13-17): LMS build, email setup, analytics, internal QA
• Soft Launch (18): Team goes through as learners, final tweaks

Key Output: Fully functional pilot ready for external learners

PILOT LAUNCH
Weeks 19-22

Run & Validate

Activities: Activate waitlist and recruit 30-50 participants | Release lessons on schedule (1-2 per day) | Run optional peer circle/QA | Moderation and support

Data Collection: Track completion, time-on-page, comments | Collect post-course survey | Conduct 5-10 participant interviews

Key Output: Real-world learning data and participant feedback

EVOLVE
Weeks 23-24

Synthesize & Decide

Activities: Analyze metrics against success criteria | Conduct team debrief retrospective | Compare outcomes to baseline | Make go/no-go decision

Deliverables: Pilot report (findings, testimonials, metrics) | Updated roadmap | Stakeholder presentation | Risk register

Key Output: Clear decision and path forward (scale, iterate, or pause)

Market Problem & Opportunity

The Gap

Mid-career professionals face fragmented support across three critical needs:

  • Career clarity & direction: Designing Your Life workshops exist but are geographically limited and cost €800-2,000
  • Deep work and focus: Cal Newport's books and essays lack practical application and live community
  • Relational health: Wellness platforms (Commune, Insight Timer) focus on meditation/trauma but miss career/productivity emphasis

Result: professionals piece together advice from books, podcasts, and coaching instead of having a structured, integrated, behavior-change platform.

Target Segment Profile

  • Affluent & educated: college/masters degree, willing to invest in self-development
  • Career-focused: managers, entrepreneurs, independent professionals
  • Seeking clarity: navigating next career move, work-life balance, authentic relationships
  • Prefer quality over quantity: curated content over infinite feeds
  • Value community: peer learning and accountability matter
  • Busy schedules: need asynchronous, flexible formats

Market Validation Signals

  • Deep Work (Cal Newport): Bestseller for 8+ years; sustained demand
  • Designing Your Life: Maintains waitlists despite high cost
  • Commune Starting Fresh: 200+ 5-star reviews; strong social proof
  • Masterclass & Maven: Cohort programs priced €99-299 sell consistently
  • Substack & Patreon: Niche creators building €5-50k/month revenue with similar audiences

Our Opportunity

Integrated Blended Model: Combine expert video (like Commune), behavior-change tools (like DYL), and deep work frameworks (like Cal Newport) into a single platform with built-in accountability, peer circles, and ongoing coaching options.

70-20-10 Learning Model: 70% experiential (tools, exercises), 20% social (peer circles, community), 10% formal (videos, essays) — proven for adult behavior change.

Sustainable Revenue: Layer courses, bundles, membership, peer circles, masterminds, 1:1 coaching, and B2B licensing to achieve profitability by Year 2.
