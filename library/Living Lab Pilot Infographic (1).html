Generate tangible results that peers can see, touch, and taste, then let success attract resources rather than seeking permission first.

Action 1: Identify and Secure Core Volunteer Team

Timeline: Week 1-3

Objective: Assemble 8-15 committed volunteers to serve as pilot project founders.

Recruitment Strategy:

  • Hyperlocal outreach: 'Door-to-door' conversations in immediate surroundings. Target: 50-100 conversations
  • Community gathering infiltration: Attend existing formal and informal meetings without interfering
  • Digital activation: Create social media groups on Signal, WhatsApp, Instagram documenting journey from Day 1
  • Skill-based recruitment: Seek diverse capabilities (2-3 subject experts, 2-3 communicators, 2-3 practical makers, 2-3 connectors, 2-3 enthusiastic learners)

Selection Criteria:

  • Resident of learning community
  • Available 4-6 hours/week for 6 months minimum
  • Comfortable with public documentation (photos, videos, social sharing)
  • Growth mindset: willing to learn, fail, adapt

Deliverable: Core team of 8-15 volunteers with signed commitment agreements. First team meeting scheduled (potluck dinner format to build social bonds)

Action 2: Select Location and Knowledge Subject

Timeline: Week 2-4

Objective: Identify knowledge subject and secure physical location for the pilot hub.

Knowledge Subject Selection Process:

  • Core volunteer team researches subject matter options
  • Ideate 5-8 potential subject matter frames
  • Collectively score each subject on 1-10 scale across criteria
  • Top 2 frames: Search for existing experts to partner

Physical Location Selection Criteria (ranked by priority):

  • Visibility: High traffic location with daily progress visibility
  • Size: Manageable for 5-10 volunteers, impressive enough to demonstrate potential
  • Accessibility: Easy entrance, no safety hazards, wheelchair-accessible paths
  • Location tenure: Willing owner/municipality contact for 1-2 year pilot agreement
  • Infrastructure: WiFi access or rainwater catchment feasibility

Deliverable: Secured physical location with formal or informal agreement. Subject matter expertise identified and preliminary expert contacts made.

Action 3: Launch Social Learning Media Strategy

Timeline: Week 3-4

Objective: Establish multi-channel documentation and community engagement system.

Digital Platforms:

  • Instagram account (@project_name): Daily photos, Stories, Reels. Target: 100 followers by Month 2, 500 by Month 6
  • Social messaging: Signal, WhatsApp groups for volunteer coordination
  • Wiki: Public view access for plans, budgets, lessons learned, photos, videos

Content Strategy:

  • Daily updates: Instagram Stories showing volunteer faces and progress shots
  • Weekly reflection posts: Longer narratives on learning outcomes with honest failure documentation
  • Monthly video summary: 3-5 min before/after transformation with volunteer testimonials
  • Monthly live Q&A sessions: Real-time peer engagement and answering questions

Offline Visibility:

  • Site signage (€100-200): Large banner at location entrance with project info and join details
  • Flyer distribution (€50): A5 flyers to 200 nearest households inviting participation
  • Info board at site: Protected bulletin board with weekly updates, photos, activity calendar

Deliverable: Active social media presence (50+ posts/month), physical signage installed, 100+ peers aware within 4 weeks

Action 4: Collaborative Knowledge Team Preparation

Timeline: Month 2

Objective: Host intensive build days to establish infrastructure and learning modules.

Community Build Days (2 full weekdays, 10 am-4 pm):

  • Week 1 - Site prep: Onboarding hybrid location with 20-30 participants
  • Week 2 - Infrastructure installation: Build knowledge hub infrastructure with hands-on learning

Learning Modules (Morning Sessions):

  • Knowledge development basics and research
  • Media production and publication formats
  • Document management and prompt engineering
  • Tool safety and process management
  • Guest speaker sessions with experienced experts

Hands-On Work (Afternoon Sessions):

  • Teams of 4-5 research knowledge and produce media (supervised)
  • Infrastructure installation with different task areas

Community Building Elements:

  • Potluck lunch to build social bonds
  • Time-lapse photography documentation
  • Volunteer interviews capturing participation motivation

Deliverable: Fully functional hybrid learning product with 10-15 modules. 40+ volunteers participated. 50+ peers aware through documentation.

Action 5: Research and Publishing Launch

Timeline: Month 3

Objective: Establish operational knowledge hub with regular maintenance and community engagement.

Community Presentation Event:

  • 10-11 am: Kick-off ceremony - volunteers share vision and invite participation
  • 11 am-1 pm: Research and publish formats led by experts (rotating every 20-30 minutes)
  • 1-2 pm: Lunch with informal networking (community potluck or donated sandwiches)
  • 2-3 pm: Maintenance training and weekly care signup

Knowledge Development Testing Strategy:

  • Early wins (30-45 days): Update learning experiences with acquired knowledge
  • Medium-term (60-90 days): Design new learning experience module and test
  • Long-term experiments (90-120 days): Become thought leader in expertise area

Weekly Maintenance Routine:

  • 2-hour research and publication maintenance sessions
  • 2-hour rapid prototyping for module design
  • Volunteer coordination (rotating responsibilities)

Deliverable: Operational knowledge hub. 15-20 active weekly volunteers. Established maintenance rhythm. First new learning experience within 30-45 days.

Action 6: Prototype Presentation Milestone Event

Timeline: Month 4

Objective: Celebrate volunteer effort, demonstrate tangible results, recruit next wave, and generate stakeholder interest.

Event Program (3 hours):

  • 2-2:30 pm: Guided presentations in small rotating groups (8-10 people per 15 min slot)
  • 2:30-3:30 pm: Product demonstration with hands-on testing
  • 3:30-4 pm: Training demonstration showing implementation recipes
  • 4-4:30 pm: Community discussion on learnings and next steps

Documentation Strategy:

  • High-quality photo and video content production
  • Testimonial collection from 10-15 participants
  • Quantitative metrics display: volunteer hours, participants, resource use
  • Media outreach targeting local news outlets and bloggers

Stakeholder Engagement:

  • Casual invitation to organization representatives (no pressure)
  • Professional documentation for stakeholder sharing
  • Relationship building for future partnerships

Deliverable: Successful event with 100-150 attendees. New training module introduced. 20-30 new volunteer recruits. 1-2 media articles/posts. Documented proof of concept for stakeholder outreach.

Action 7: Launch Second Knowledge Hub

Timeline: Month 5

Objective: Expand model through organic growth and volunteer leadership.

Organic Growth Strategy:

  • Leverage pilot success to secure additional resources
  • Ask expanded volunteer base (30-40 people) which knowledge hub to create next
  • Select site using proximity and template from pilot
  • Test different processes and formats for diversification

Rapid Replication Process:

  • Two-day intensive blitz: Experienced volunteers lead newcomer teams
  • Shared resources: Borrow tools, share processes, use proven designs
  • Video documentation: Create "How we built a hub in 48 hours" tutorial for replication

Cost Efficiency:

  • Lower budget than Pilot 1 (€500-1,500) due to learning curve and material recycling
  • Demonstrates replicability and scalability

Deliverable: Second functional hub operational within 4-6 weeks. 40-50 total active volunteers across 2 sites. Replication model proven.

Action 8: Establish Distributed Learning Hub Model

Timeline: Month 5-6

Objective: Transition from single hub to networked learning ecosystem.

Create "Knowledge Champions" Leadership Structure:

  • Each site has 2-3 Site Champions stepping into coordination roles
  • Monthly Champions Meetings (rotating locations) for knowledge sharing
  • Optional stipend (€50-100/month) for Champions recognizing time commitment

Cross-Site Learning Events:

  • Monthly skill workshops: Rotating topics and locations (composting, seed saving, publication strategies, crop planning)
  • Harvest swaps: Sites exchange surplus produce and knowledge
  • Volunteer exchange days: Cross-pollinate knowledge between sites

Digital Platform Evolution:

  • "Lessons Learned" section documenting honest failures and solutions
  • Video tutorial library (2-5 min how-to videos on key skills)
  • Open-source replication toolkit (downloadable PDF guide)

Deliverable: Networked learning system with 2 active hubs. 40-50 volunteers. Monthly cross-site events. Public knowledge base. Scalable model demonstrated.

Action 9: Initiate Stakeholder Engagement

Timeline: Month 6-7

Objective: Approach institutions AFTER demonstrating viability (strategic timing).

Wave 1: Resource Partners (Month 6):

  • Target: Businesses, universities, research institutions with low bureaucracy
  • Approach: Invitation-based casual visits. "Come see what we're building"
  • Ask: Small specific requests (donations, student internships, mentorship)

Wave 2: Training and Educational Institutions (Month 7):

  • Target: Training organizations, coaches, trainers
  • Approach: Present completed pilot results (1-page professional brief)
  • Ask: Collaboration on platform to publish and monetize knowledge
  • Business models: Sell knowledge through Amazon/Udemy, Substack/Patreon, or product recommendations

Wave 3: Funding Partners (Month 8+):

  • Target: Foundations, impact investors (ING, Triodos Bank), EU grants
  • Approach: Formal funding applications with pilot data
  • Ask: €20-50K for 3-year expansion plan (5-10 hubs, 100-150 volunteers)

Deliverable: 3-5 initial partnerships established. €5-15K secured in grants/donations. Municipal relationship building initiated. Positioned for major scaling.

Action 10: Launch "Adopt-a-Hub" Program

Timeline: Month 9

Objective: Enable external replication and distributed scaling beyond core team.

Program Implementation:

  • Recruit 10-15 adapters from expanded volunteer base and event attendees
  • Provide adoption kits with processes, architecture, and instructions
  • Host training workshop on hub setup, knowledge selection, municipal guidelines
  • Offer ongoing support group for troubleshooting and problem-solving

Learning Documentation:

  • Video documentaries tracking each hub transformation (before → 1 month → 3 months → 6 months)
  • Simple yield logging: views, trainings given, presentations delivered
  • Neighbor feedback surveys on knowledge, connection, and community impact

Support Structure:

  • Monthly "hub tours" where adopters visit each other's hubs
  • Expert advisory support group for peer troubleshooting
  • Recognition and celebration of adopter achievements

Deliverable: 10-15 productive hubs managed by trained adapters. Ultra-low-cost (€500-1,500 budget). Distributed model proven. External scalability demonstrated.

Action 11: Develop Replication Toolkit and Starter Kit

Timeline: Month 10

Objective: Create open-source implementation package enabling independent external scaling.

Physical Starter Kit (for new sites):

  • Materials list with exact sources and retail locations
  • Tool library inventory with minimum tools (15 items, €300 budget)
  • Knowledge recommendations with proven successful formats

Digital Toolkit (freely downloadable):

  • Step-by-step guide (20-30 pages): Week-by-week implementation timeline
  • Budget templates: Actual pilot costs, revenue tracking, volunteer hour valuation
  • Legal templates: Agreements, waivers, governance bylaws
  • Communication templates: Social media posts, flyers, press releases
  • Video library: 10-15 tutorials on all key skills

Distribution Strategy:

  • Host replication workshop for 5-10 organizations interested in model
  • Submit toolkit to professional networks and academic groups
  • Publish on GitHub (technical) and simple website (general public)

Deliverable: Complete replication toolkit published online. 50+ downloads. 3-5 neighborhoods express adaptation interest. Model scalability proven.

Action 12: Launch Distribution System and Financial Sustainability

Timeline: Month 11-12

Objective: Transition from production to sustainable business model ensuring long-term viability.

Monthly Knowledge Market (Ongoing):

  • Product offering: Fresh knowledge outputs, publications, courses, applications, sensors, recommended products
  • Pricing model: Donation-based "pay what you can" (suggested €2-5 per item)
  • Staffing: 3-4 volunteers per market on rotating schedule
  • Scaling: Month 1 target 30-50 customers; Month 3 target 75-100 customers

Subscription Box Pilot (Month 12):

  • Offering: Weekly curated knowledge upgrades and learning materials
  • Target: 20-30 subscribers
  • Revenue: €160-360/week = €640-1,440/month
  • Impact: Predictable income, customer loyalty, expansion capital

Organization Partnerships (Month 12):

  • Target: 2-3 organizations interested in specific knowledge development
  • Offer: Regular delivery of curated knowledge and learning experiences
  • Revenue: €50-150/week per partner
  • Benefit: Premium pricing, consistent demand, strong marketing story

Financial Sustainability Targets:

  • €1,500-3,000/month revenue by end of Year 1
  • Monthly market revenues: €300-600
  • Subscription box revenues: €640-1,440
  • Organization partnership revenues: €450-1,800

Deliverable: Operational knowledge distribution system generating sustainable revenue. Professional credibility demonstrated. Financial viability pathway established for Year 2+ expansion.

Success = Tangible Impact Demonstrated → Resource Attraction → Organic Growth

The pilot prioritizes learning, transparency, and community engagement. Success is measured not just in deliverables, but in volunteer commitment, peer engagement, and the ability to replicate the model independently.
