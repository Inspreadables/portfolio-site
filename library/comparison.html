⚖️ Platform Agnosticism

Substack and Patreon profit from the volume of transactions. Their risk is commoditization; if a cheaper tool arrives, creators migrate. They must build "Network Effects" (like Substack Notes) to prevent leakage.

🎓 Authority Verticality

Designing Your Life and School of Life own the "Mental Model." You can't get DYL tools anywhere else. Their risk is brand fatigue, which they combat through multi-format integration (Books, Apps, Coaching).

Revenue Resilience vs. Fees

Platforms rely on a small "tax" (5-10%) on others' success. Authority models rely on high-margin IP licensing and memberships. As platforms mature, they often face "The Leakage Problem" where top earners leave to avoid fees.

Vertical Integration: The Commune Advantage

Commune controls the content creation, the hosting, and the community. By eliminating middleman fees, their net margin on a $20/mo subscription is roughly 75%, compared to Substack's 10% take-rate.

The Certification Flywheel: DYL

Designing Your Life scales by selling "Licenses to Teach." This turns their biggest fans into a decentralized sales force, creating revenue that platforms can never access.
