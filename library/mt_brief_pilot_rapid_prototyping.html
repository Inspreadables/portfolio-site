Human‑centered design meets strategic growth. Explore the three core phases based on
IDEO’s Design Thinking & De Baak huisstijl v2.0 — insights, methods, and tools.

Empathy & Research (Primary)
Action & Prototyping (Secondary)
Wild Ideas & Creativity (Accent)

Empathy interviews

Deep, one‑on‑one conversations with students, teachers & parents to uncover unspoken needs. Observe behaviors, not just opinions.

🎯 method · user stories

Analogous immersion

Seek inspiration outside education — visit a design studio, hospital lobby or tech hub. Fresh perspectives unlock hidden opportunities.

🌍 lateral learning

Stakeholder mapping

Visualize relationships & influence. Identify fans, skeptics and silent advocates to build a shared understanding of the ecosystem.

🗺️ systemic view

Research synthesis

Cluster observations into themes & patterns. Turn raw data into actionable “How Might We” opportunities.

📊 sense‑making

Brainstorming rules

Defer judgment, encourage wild ideas, build on others, go for quantity. 100 ideas in 60 minutes — quantity unlocks quality.

💥 IDEO method

How Might We (HMW)

Reframe problems as opportunity questions. “HMW transform curriculum delivery?” — broad yet focused ideation fuel.

✨ reframing tool

Visual sketch & storyboard

Draw ideas instead of just writing. Stick figures & diagrams clarify complex solutions and spark unexpected iterations.

✏️ low‑fidelity

Idea selection matrix

Vote on most promising concepts. Use passion + feasibility criteria to shortlist ideas for rapid prototyping.

📌 dot‑voting

Low‑fidelity prototyping

Build anything: paper mock-ups, role‑play, storyboards. The goal is to learn, not to be perfect. Fail early, iterate fast.

🧪 rapid experiments

Feedback loops

Share prototypes with real users. Ask: “What would you change?” Observe body language. Capture verbatim quotes.

🔄 user‑driven

Iterative refinement

Integrate feedback, drop features that confuse, amplify what delights. Each prototype cycle increases relevance.

⚡ build‑measure‑learn

Scaling & storytelling

Document success, pitch to stakeholders, build partnerships. Turn grassroots solutions into systemic change.
