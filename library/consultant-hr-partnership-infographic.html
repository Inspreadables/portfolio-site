Framework 1

The Consultant Approach Framework

Expert-validated steps for external L&D consultants approaching HR departments

1

Do Your Homework

Research the organization thoroughly before first contact. Understand their culture, challenges, strategic priorities, and language.

"The first thing a trainer should do is familiarize themselves with the culture of the organization they're approaching — before the sales call." — Training Industry
2

Lead with Curiosity

Ask questions rather than pitching solutions. Understand their real needs, what they're measured against, and how they define success.

"Slow the process down. Ask some more questions and get nosy. One of the best attributes in L&D is curiosity." — Krystyna Gadd
3

Speak Their Language

Move away from "L&D speak" and use business terminology. Address their priorities, operational pains, and strategic objectives.

"L&D leaders need to speak the language of business. Customers need to know that you understand the pains of operation." — ATD Research
4

Co-Create Solutions

Don't jump into "solutions mode" too quickly. Build trust through collaboration and ensure stakeholders become champions of the initiative.

"Teams carefully negotiate issues of power and authority when deciding on which problems of practice to take up." — DBIR Principles
Framework 2

Stakeholder Engagement Levels

Choose the right engagement approach for each HR stakeholder

📢

Inform

Provide balanced, objective information. One-way communication for awareness.

💬

Consult

Solicit feedback on analysis and decisions. Limited two-way exchange.

🔄

Involve

Work directly throughout process. Ensure concerns are consistently addressed.

🤝

Collaborate

Partner in design and decision-making. Shared ownership of outcomes.

Framework 3

Building Your Value Proposition

The three questions every consultant must answer for HR clients

1

Why Change?

What business problem does your collaboration solve? Quantify the cost of the current state.

2

Change to What?

What does success look like? Define measurable outcomes aligned with stakeholder metrics.

3

Why You?

What unique expertise do you bring? Demonstrate understanding of their specific situation.

Implementation

Consultant-HR Partnership Roadmap

A 30-week journey from first contact to sustainable partnership

Phase 1

Discovery & Engagement

Weeks 1-4
  • Research organization & stakeholders
  • Initial meetings with HR leadership
  • Needs assessment & gap analysis
  • Map stakeholder requirements
Phase 2

Proposal & Partnership

Weeks 5-10
  • Develop value proposition
  • Create business case with ROI
  • Co-design solution with HR
  • Secure stakeholder sign-off
Phase 3

Pilot & Iteration

Weeks 11-18
  • Launch with 50-100 employees
  • Collect multi-level data
  • Joint sensemaking with HR
  • Iterate based on evidence
Phase 4

Scale & Deliver

Weeks 19-26
  • Phased organization rollout
  • Train internal champions
  • Establish governance with HR
  • Document success stories
Phase 5

Sustain & Expand

Week 27+
  • Measure and report ROI to HR
  • Quarterly partnership reviews
  • Identify expansion opportunities
  • Build long-term relationship
